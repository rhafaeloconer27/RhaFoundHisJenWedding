Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful Wedding Details

Have Questions?

We have gathered the most important details to help you prepare for our wedding celebration.

What time should I arrive?

The ceremony will begin promptly at 3:00 PM. Kindly arrive 30 minutes early to allow enough time for seating.

Where will the ceremony and reception take place?

Both the ceremony and reception will be held at:

Jhaymar’s Treehouse Events Place 29 Gloria Street, Barangay Gulod, Novaliches, Quezon City

Can I bring a plus one?

Due to limited seating, we respectfully ask that only the guests specifically listed on the invitation attend.

Are gifts expected?

Your presence at our wedding is the greatest gift. However, if you wish to bless us with a gift, a monetary gift to help us begin our new life together would be deeply appreciated.

Can I bring my children?

While we love your little ones, this will be an adult-focused celebration unless your child is specifically included in the invitation.

Who can I contact for questions?

If you have any questions regarding the wedding, RSVP, venue, or other details, please contact the Bride, Groom, or our Wedding Coordinator.

Is parking available at the venue?

Yes. Parking is available at Jhaymar’s Treehouse Events Place for our guests.

What should I do if I arrive late?

If you arrive after the ceremony has started, our ushers will guide you to your seat during an appropriate moment.

Will there be a seating arrangement?

Yes. There will be a designated seating arrangement during the reception. Our ushers will gladly assist you in finding your assigned table.

Can I take photos during the ceremony?

We kindly request an unplugged ceremony. Please refrain from taking photos during the ceremony so everyone can fully enjoy the moment. Our official photographers and videographers will capture these memories for us.

What should I wear?

Please follow our Modern Grayscale Elegance wedding theme.

Charcoal Slate Gray Gray Silver Light Gray

How can you help make our wedding memorable?

  • Please RSVP before the deadline.
  • Arrive at least 30 minutes early.
  • Dress according to our wedding theme.
  • Please avoid walking across the aisle during the ceremony.
  • Stay until the end of the celebration and enjoy the program with us.

Thank you for taking the time to read our wedding reminders. We are excited to celebrate this special day with you.
